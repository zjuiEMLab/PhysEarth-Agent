# cmem-sampling-density figure assets

These figure assets are publisher-provided figures for:

Lv, S., Schalge, B., Saavedra Garfias, P., and Simmer, C. (2020), “Required sampling density of ground-based soil moisture and brightness temperature observations for calibration and validation of L-band satellite observations based on a virtual reality,” *Hydrology and Earth System Sciences*, 24, 1957–1973,
https://doi.org/10.5194/hess-24-1957-2020.

The publisher serves this article's figures as PNG only; no vector PDF is available, so
`onboarding/extract_figure_metadata.py` finds no axis or legend text to extract and the card
carries the caption alone. The article and its figures are distributed under the CC BY 4.0
license: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.

They are paper figures, not model output and not digitized numerical data. `card.yaml`
stores the caption, source URL, source format, asset path and paper section for each asset.
