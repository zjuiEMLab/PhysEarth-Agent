PhysEarth-Agent

An LLM agent that configures physical Earth models with justified confidence, and what it needs to become a conference paper

Audit of yiwenff/PhysEarth-Agent at 35eee2c

12 minutes · 20 slides

~30 s. PhysEarth-Agent turns registered geophysical models (SMRT, tau-omega, water cloud, PROSAIL, pyET, pywatershed) into instruments an LLM can operate, with the checks built into the system rather than the prompt. This talk: what it is, what the current evidence shows, what the audit found, and a path to a publishable paper, including more reproduction cases and plugins for Claude Code, Codex and DeepSeek-backed agents.
