~50 s. The science coverage. Three models answer what a surface looks like to a microwave sensor: SMRT for snow, tau-omega for passive emission from vegetated soil, the water cloud model for Sentinel-1 style backscatter. PROSAIL answers what the same vegetated surface looks like to an optical sensor. pyET and pywatershed answer what the water is doing, down to a five-year PRMS run over the official Sagehen Creek domain. The point for the science audience: one set of checks covers a model that never emits a photon as well as a radiative-transfer model, and choices that matter physically, which theory with which microstructure or which evapotranspiration formulation, are declared on the card rather than left to the LLM's memory.
