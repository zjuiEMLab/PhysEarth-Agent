~50 s. The one task with committed records: reproduce Figure 3 from a natural-language question, using the six legal theory and microstructure combinations. Qualitatively it works: six curves, correct ordering, the non-sticky pair saturates. Read the right edge though: sticky DMRT ends near 0.019 where the paper has about 0.025. That is not an agent error. The paper states neither stickiness nor frequency for this figure; the agent used the card defaults, 0.2 and 37 GHz, while the authors' own notebook uses 0.15 and 36.5 GHz. Scored against the notebook, four curves are within 3 to 4 percent, all of it the frequency, and the two sticky curves are 12 percent off. Either choice is legitimate; what matters is that the report says it was a default, which is slide 13. Also note the plotting style collapses the IBA variants to one dark dashed colour, so the family pairing that the paper's colour scheme carries is lost.
